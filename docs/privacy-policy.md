# Privacy Policy for Post Spatial

**Last Updated: March 15, 2026**

## Overview

Post Spatial ("Post", "we", "our") is a spatial messaging application that allows users to anchor digital notes to real-world locations using augmented reality technology.

## Data We Collect

### Location Data
Post collects your device's GPS coordinates to anchor messages to physical locations. Location data in debug logs is rounded to 4 decimal places (approximately 11 meters of precision). We do not store your location with more precision than is necessary for the app to function.

### Camera Data
Post accesses your device camera to enable the augmented reality experience. Camera data is processed entirely on your device using Apple's ARKit and Google's ARCore SDK. Camera images are never uploaded to our servers or stored.

### Messages
Messages you create are stored on our servers and associated with the GPS coordinates where they were placed. Messages are visible to other Post users who visit the same location.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect personal identification information beyond what is required for the app to function.
- We do not track your browsing history.
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.
- We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

## Third-Party Services

Post uses the following third-party services:
- **Google ARCore Geospatial API**: Processes visual data on-device to determine your precise location relative to buildings and landmarks.
- **Google Maps**: Displays the map view within the app.
- **Firebase**: Used for app distribution and analytics.

## Data Retention

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the support link below.
