Three steps to drop your first post

1

Step outside and point your phone

Open Post and point your camera at any building. The VPS accuracy badge turns cyan once the system locks on — usually 2–5 seconds outdoors.

2

Hold to charge, release to drop

A reticle snaps to the building surface. Long-press to charge your pin. Release and type your message. It's anchored to that exact spot in the world.

3

Others discover it walking by

Anyone with Post who walks within range will see your pin floating on the building in AR. The city becomes a canvas.

Built on real spatial technology

No marker-based tricks. No QR codes. No pre-mapped spaces.

ARCore Geospatial

Google Visual Positioning System

Matches the visual features of real buildings against Google Street View data to determine your exact position and orientation.

StreetscapeGeometry

3D Building Mesh

Real-time 3D geometry of building facades lets pins anchor to surfaces with sub-meter accuracy, not just coordinates.

GeoHash Indexing

Instant Spatial Queries

9-tile GeoHash fan-out queries find every post within your radius in milliseconds regardless of how many posts exist globally.

Firebase + Cloud Run

Production Backend

Every post is written through an authenticated backend API — never directly to the database. Built to scale from day one.
