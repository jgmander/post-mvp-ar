Delete Your Account

You can delete your Post Spatial account and associated data at any time using the steps below. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

📱 Delete from the App

  1. Open Post Spatial on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm when prompted.

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

✉️ Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, send a deletion request to our support team. Include the email address or user ID associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

📧 Email privacy@get-post.co

🗑️ What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Type Action on Deletion Retention Period
Firebase account (UID, email) Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Posts you created (text, location) Permanently deleted Within 30 days
Reports you filed against other content Reporter ID anonymised Retained for safety audit (90 days), then purged
Aggregated, anonymised analytics Retained anonymised Indefinite (no personal identifiers)

🗂️ Delete Individual Posts (Without Deleting Account)

  1. Open Post Spatial and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap any post you have created.
  3. Tap the Delete icon (trash can) and confirm.

Individual posts are deleted immediately and permanently from all users' views.
