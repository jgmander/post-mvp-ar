Child Safety Standards

Post Spatial is committed to the safety of all users, especially minors. This page describes our standards and practices against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Applies to: Post Spatial (Android & iOS)

🚫 Zero-Tolerance Policy

Post Spatial has a zero-tolerance policy for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any content that sexually exploits, endangers, or sexualizes minors. Any such content is immediately removed and reported to the appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline.

🛡️ Detection & Prevention

  • AI content screening: All user-generated posts are screened by Google Gemini AI before publication. Content that is unsafe, exploitative, or harmful is blocked and never stored.
  • Age-appropriate design: The app is rated 12+ and is not designed for or marketed to children under 13. Features that connect strangers are location-limited and do not include private messaging.
  • No direct messaging: Post Spatial does not support private or direct messages between users, reducing vectors for grooming or exploitation.
  • No image or video uploads: Users cannot upload photos or videos, eliminating a primary medium for CSAM distribution.
  • Anonymous browsing limited: Creating posts requires authentication, ensuring accountability for published content.

🚩 In-App Reporting

Post Spatial provides visible and accessible reporting tools for all users:

  • A Report button is available on every post in both the AR view and the map view. Tapping Report flags the content immediately.
  • Reported content is automatically re-analyzed by Gemini AI. Content confirmed as violating is auto-removed; ambiguous cases are escalated for manual review within 24 hours.
  • Users can also Hide any post locally so it never appears for them again.

⚖️ Response & Legal Compliance

  • NCMEC CyberTipline: Any confirmed or suspected CSAM is reported to NCMEC as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A.
  • Law enforcement cooperation: Post Spatial cooperates fully with law enforcement investigations involving child safety, including preserving records and responding to lawful legal process.
  • GDPR / COPPA compliance: Post Spatial does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Accounts identified as belonging to minors under 13 are removed upon discovery.
  • Account removal: Users or accounts found sharing CSAM or exploitative content are permanently banned and reported.

📬 Child Safety Contact

To report child safety concerns, contact our designated safety officer directly. We aim to respond to all child safety reports within 24 hours.

📧 safety@get-post.co Designated Safety Contact

For urgent reports involving imminent danger, contact local law enforcement or NCMEC's CyberTipline at missingkids.org/cybertipline.

✅ In-App Child Safety Reporting

Post Spatial provides the ability for users to report child safety concerns directly within the app using the Report function available on all posts. Reports are reviewed promptly and escalated to authorities when required.

These standards are reviewed and updated at least annually or whenever significant product changes are made. For questions, contact safety@get-post.co.
